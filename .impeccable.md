# Impeccable — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
- B2C: Portuguese home-buyers (30–55) comparing mortgage/credit options — anxious about a
  high-stakes financial decision, browsing evenings on phone or desktop. Need reassurance,
  clarity, zero jargon. Conversion = simulation request / contact form.
- B2B: real-estate agents & partner businesses evaluating a referral partnership. Need
  credibility, scale proof (group stats), fast path to contact.
- Regulated industry: Banco de Portugal registration must remain visible verbatim (footer).

### Brand Personality
Warm, trustworthy, golden-hour optimism. Three words: **warm, solid, ascendant**.
"Amanhecer" (dawn) — the feeling of a sunrise over a calm horizon: hope + stability.
NOT: fintech-neon, corporate-cold, startup-playful.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Tokens: ink #1D1D1B (never pure black), warm paper #FAF7F2, ember #F39200 (primary
  accent), amber ≈#FFB100, bronze #A07214. Montserrat for ALL UI text; Brown Sugar is
  logo-only. OKLCH-friendly warm neutrals tinted toward ember.
- Two switchable directions (prototype): **Cinema** (dark golden-hour, page "dawns"
  toward the footer sunrise) and **Editorial** (warm-paper financial magazine).
- The brand sun appears ONLY inside the actual logo lockup — never as standalone page
  ornament (user decision, Jun 2026).
- Motion: framer-motion line-mask reveals + fades, exponential ease, reduced-motion safe.
  One signature scroll moment: the pinned Process deck.
- Anti-references: generic AI fintech slop (cyan/purple gradients, glass cards, glow
  borders), Decisões e Soluções' dated corporate look, identical card grids.

### Design Principles
1. Same copy, same functionality — presentation only (forms, wizard, simulator, ids).
2. Warmth over flash: ember accents are rare and earned (60-30-10).
3. Numbers are heroes (stats), but aligned and calm — credibility over cleverness.
4. Every reveal must fail safe: content visible even if animation never fires.
5. Regulatory + trust elements (BdP, ANICA) are sacred — always legible, never decorative.
