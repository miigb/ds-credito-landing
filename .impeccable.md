# .impeccable.md

## Design Context

### Users
Two audiences. **(1) Credit-seeking individuals/families in Portugal** — often
stressed about a mortgage, consolidation, or financing decision; they arrive
anxious and need to feel they're in expert, trustworthy hands. **(2) The partner /
niche network** (real-estate agents, solicitadores, auto dealers, etc.) who refer
clients and need to see a credible, premium operator. Context: mostly mobile, often
researching at home in the evening. Job-to-be-done: "help me understand my options
and trust someone to handle the bank for me." Regulated entity (Banco de Portugal
nº 0007470) — claims must be sober and compliant.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **warm, dawning, reassuring**. (Not "modern", not "elegant" — those are
dead categories.) The brand is a *sunrise*: the moment a difficult financial
situation turns around. Premium but human; optimistic without hype; calm authority,
never aggressive sales. Emotional goal: relief + confidence + hope.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial, cinematic, warm. Identity = a rising-sun mark with radiating rays.
Theme: **dark** for the hero/cinematic surfaces — derived from use-case (a
darkness→dawn video; the mark needs night to rise from; glow reproduces the real
backlit office sign), NOT a default. Light editorial surfaces are acceptable for
lower content sections. Reference: the physical backlit gold signage
(`public/brand-2026/reference/office-signage.jpeg`) and the manual's warm
golden-hour mood photography. Anti-references: the OLD site's cosmic-purple /
magenta "Midnight Curator" system; generic fintech dark-mode-with-neon-glow;
AI-slop (gradient text, side-stripe cards, glassmorphism everywhere, centered
stat-card grids).

### Design Principles
1. **The sun is the system.** Rays, dawn, warm light — every motif traces to the
   mark. Glow is purposeful (it's real backlit light), never decorative.
2. **One accent, earned.** Ember Gold `#F39200` is THE accent; Sun Gold + Bronze
   are its family. 60-30-10. Palette in OKLCH; neutrals tinted warm, never cold
   slate; no pure `#000`/`#fff`; zero purple/magenta.
3. **Editorial, not templated.** Asymmetric, left-aligned, generous rhythm. No
   identical card grids, no centered-everything, no hero-stat template.
4. **Type does the talking.** BrownSugar display (brand) vs Montserrat labels
   (brand). Few sizes, high contrast (≥1.3). Banned reflex fonts (Fraunces,
   Playfair, Cormorant, Inter, …) must never substitute the display face.
5. **Animate the arrival, then be still.** One signature motion (the sunrise);
   no idle micro-animations competing. Transform/opacity only; no bounce/elastic;
   honour `prefers-reduced-motion`.
6. **Pass the AI-slop test.** If someone could say "an AI made this" and be
   believed, it's not done.
